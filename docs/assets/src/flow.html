N1X Cortex · what it does

From a folder of files to answers that cite their source.

Cortex reads what you already have, turns it into small linked notes that each point at their source, and answers from those notes, for you and for your agents.

01
Your files

A folder of markdown, or a repository with no docs at all. The originals are never modified.

02
A cited note graph

One idea per note, linked by wikilinks and typed by frontmatter. Every note points at the document it came from.

03
Answers that name their source

You and your agents ask in plain language. Every answer comes back with the notes it stands on.
