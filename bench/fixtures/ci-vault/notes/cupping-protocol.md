title: Cupping Protocol
type: process
status: curated
---

Cupping uses 8.25 g of coffee per 150 ml of water at 93 C. The crust is broken
at 4 minutes. Scores are recorded on a 100-point scale; anything at or above 84
is designated specialty. Samples are roasted to a light [[roast-profile]].
